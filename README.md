## Favorite Series: JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

"Yare yare daze..." (Good grief...) — Jotaro Kujo

### About
*JoJo's Bizarre Adventure* is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by *Hirohiko Araki*. It started in 1987 and later became a hugely popular anime. The story follows the *Joestar family* across generations, where each part stars a new "JoJo" facing off against powerful enemies. In *Stardust Crusaders* (Part 3), *Jotaro Kujo* awakens a *Stand*, a spirit-like fighting power, and travels with his allies, the *Stardust Crusaders*, to Egypt to defeat the vampire *DIO*.

### Main Stardust Crusaders
1. Jotaro Kujo – Star Platinum
2. Joseph Joestar – Hermit Purple
3. Muhammad Avdol – Magician's Red
4. Noriaki Kakyoin – Hierophant Green
5. Jean Pierre Polnareff – Silver Chariot
6. Iggy – The Fool

### Why I Love It
- Creative and unpredictable battles
- Strong themes of *family*, *destiny*, and *courage*
- Iconic poses, bold art style, and memorable catchphrases
- Wild characters and unforgettable villains

## Favorite Group: BTS

"Borahae!" (I purple you) — a phrase created by V, used by fans to say "I love you"

### About
*BTS* (Bangtan Sonyeondan) is a South Korean boy band formed in Seoul. They debuted on June 13, 2013 under *Big Hit Entertainment* (now part of *HYBE*). The group has seven members who write and produce much of their own music, and they grew from a small company's rookies into one of the biggest music acts in the world. Their dedicated fanbase is called *ARMY*.

### Members
1. RM – Leader, Rapper
2. Jin – Vocalist
3. Suga – Rapper
4. J-Hope – Rapper, Dancer
5. Jimin – Vocalist, Dancer
6. V – Vocalist
7. Jungkook – Main Vocalist

### Why I Love Them
- Powerful performances and sharp choreography
- Meaningful themes of *self-love*, *youth*, and *perseverance*
- A mix of genres, from hip-hop to pop
- Strong bond with their fans, the *ARMY*
